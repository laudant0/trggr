YOUR DATA

Privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

We collect what the app needs to work, we keep your votes private, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and data whenever you want.

What we collect

From Google sign-in: your email address, name and Google profile picture. From you: your display name, bio, profile photo, whether your profile is discoverable, the polls you create and their images, your votes, pins, follows, Window notes, and reports you file. Automatically: basic technical logs from our hosting and database providers, such as IP address and browser type, used for security and abuse prevention.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your profile, run polls and results, connect members, send in-app notifications, prevent spam and abuse, and keep the service secure. We do not use your data for advertising.

What other people can see

Your individual votes are never shown to other members. Your profile and polls are visible to others only if you turn on Discoverable. Your follower and following numbers are visible only to you. Window notes are visible only to you and your followers. Poll results are shown as totals and percentages.

Who we share data with

We use Supabase to store data and handle sign-in, Google to authenticate you, and Netlify to host the website. They process data only to provide those services. We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to save poll drafts on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile, votes, pins, follows, notes and notifications. Polls you created stay up without your name. Backups may hold copies for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, switch your profile to private, remove your photo, delete your notes, unfollow members, and delete your account from the Profile page. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access or correct your data or object to how it is used; contact us and we will help.

Children

TRGGA is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to the database is controlled by row-level security, and admin tools are limited to approved admins. No system is perfectly secure, so please report anything suspicious.

Changes

If we make important changes we will update the date above.
