YOUR DATA

Privacy

We collect only what the app needs.

What we store

Your Google sign-in email and name, your profile (display name, bio, photo), your votes, pins, follows, polls and Window notes.

What others see

Your votes are private. Your profile and polls are visible to others only if you make your profile discoverable. Follower and following counts are visible only to you. Window notes are visible only to you and your followers.

Sign-in

We use Google sign-in through Supabase. We never see your Google password.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, votes, pins, follows and notes. Polls you created stay up anonymously.

Third parties

Data is stored with Supabase and the site is hosted on Netlify. We do not sell your data.
