THE RULES

Terms of use

TRGGA is a place to ask and answer polls. By using it you agree to these terms.

Be decent

No hate, harassment, threats, sexual content involving minors, illegal content, spam or impersonation. Polls and notes that break these rules can be hidden or removed, and accounts can be suspended.

Your content

You keep ownership of the polls, images and notes you post, and give TRGGA permission to display them in the app. Polls you created remain after you delete your account, with your name removed.

Fair voting

One vote per poll per member. Do not use bots, scripts or extra accounts to manipulate results.

No guarantees

TRGGA is provided as is. Poll results reflect members who voted and are not scientific surveys.

Contact

Report a poll with the Report button in the feed. For anything else, contact the TRGGA team through the site owner.
