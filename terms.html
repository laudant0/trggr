Terms of use

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

TRGGA is a community polling app where members ask questions and vote. By signing in or using the app you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

Your account

You sign in with Google. You are responsible for activity on your account. You must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, to use TRGGA. One person, one account: do not create extra accounts to vote more than once or to dodge a suspension.

Community rules

Do not post hate speech, harassment, threats, doxxing, sexual content, content involving minors in any sexual way, graphic violence, scams, spam, malware, or anything illegal. Do not impersonate other people or organisations. Do not upload images you do not have the right to use. Polls should ask a real question that members can answer.

Your content

You own the polls, images, bios and notes you post. You give TRGGA a worldwide, non-exclusive licence to store and display that content inside the app so the service can work. Polls you created remain visible if you delete your account, but your name is removed from them.

Fair voting

Each member can vote once per poll. Do not use bots, scripts, vote trading or multiple accounts to manipulate results. Results show only the members who voted, they are not scientific surveys, and they should not be treated as professional advice or fact.

Reporting and moderation

Use the Report link in the footer to tell us about a poll, take, member or problem, with a link and details. The TRGGA team reviews reports and may hide content while we look into it. The team can hide or delete polls, remove notes, and suspend accounts that break these rules. Decisions are made at our discretion, and we try to be fair.

Official polls

Polls marked OFFICIAL, such as the Daily Pulse, are published by the TRGGA team. They follow the same voting rules as everything else.

Timed polls

Some polls have a closing time. Once a poll closes, no more votes are accepted and the final results stay visible.

Privacy

How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy page, which forms part of these terms.

Availability

We work hard to keep TRGGA running, but it is provided as is and as available. We may change, pause or remove features. To the extent allowed by law, TRGGA is not liable for losses that come from using the app or from content posted by members.

Ending your account

You can delete your account any time from Profile, then Delete my account. We may suspend or remove accounts that seriously or repeatedly break these rules.

Changes to these terms

If we make important changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use TRGGA after a change means you accept it.
