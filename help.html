Help & FAQ

Quick answers about how TRGGA works.

How do I vote?

Sign in with Google, open any poll and tap an option. You get one vote per poll and it can't be changed.

Can people see how I voted?

No. Your individual votes stay private. Others only see totals, and polls you create show results to you in Profile.

What are takes?

After you vote you can add one short take (100 characters) to a poll. Members can agree with takes, and the three most agreed show first.

What are timed and official polls?

Timed polls close automatically and then show final results. Official polls, like the Daily Pulse, come from the TRGGA team.

How do I start a poll?

Tap Start a poll, ask your question, add two to four options (with optional images) and publish. Poll creation is rate-limited to keep the room tidy.

How do I follow or block someone?

Open a member's profile from Community. Follow keeps up with their polls; Block hides you from each other and removes follows both ways. Manage blocks in Profile.

How do I add my X or Instagram account?

In Profile, add your handles under Your details. It appears on your public profile only if you are in the member directory.

How do I report a poll, take or member?

Use the Report page, paste the link and explain what happened.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account. See our Privacy page for what is removed.
